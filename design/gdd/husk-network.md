## 1. Overview

The Husks remember the raider. Every raid records what the raider does to
them and where; between raids that memory reshapes how they behave and what
they build topside. A raider who leaves Husks alone gets let past; one who
starts fights is met with wider berths, shorter patience and, eventually,
shots on sight. The memory is saved in the raider's profile.

**DESIGN CALL (delegated): this is Scrapfall's core pillar.** The learning,
neutral Husk is what sets the game apart from other extraction shooters, so
changes elsewhere must not flatten it.

## 3. Detailed Design

### 3.1 What is recorded

| Counter | When |
|---|---|
| `raids` | Each raid start |
| `seen` | A neutral Husk first notices the raider |
| `peaceful` | A neutral Husk that noticed the raider stands down never having fought, or is still unfought at raid end |
| `provoked` | The raider strikes a neutral, non-hostile Husk first |
| `hits`, `crits` | Every hit on a Husk; eye hits |
| `kills[type]` | Every kill, plus `longKills` (> 35 m), `closeKills` (< 6 m), `melee`, `nades` |
| `pods`, `wrecks` | Husk pods and wrecks looted |
| `zones["zx,zz"] = [heat, calm]` | 60 m map zones: provocation +2 heat, kill +1 heat, peace +1 calm |

At each raid start heat decays ×0.85 and calm ×0.9; zones under 0.2 total are
forgotten. At most 100 zones are kept. Saved values are clamped on load.

### 3.2 Traits (recomputed each raid)

See Formulas. They drive the AI (`husk-ai.md` §3.5) and the Raider Log text.

### 3.3 Infrastructure

Each raid the network places `count` structures on the world's candidate sites:

- **Bulwarks** (sentinel spire inside curved barricades) on the sites with the
  most heat. Manned by a Watcher, plus a Buzzer if `aggression > 0.5`.
- **Cradles** (pod nurseries) on the sites with the most calm. Tended by 2
  Skitters; their Husk pods are always stocked.

### 3.4 Raider Log

The Sanctuary's Raider Log shows a mood (Hostile on sight / Wary of you /
Tolerant of you / Cautious) and plain-language lines for each active counter.

## 4. Formulas

```
kills       = sum of kills[type]
enc         = seen + 4
aggression  = clamp01((provoked + 0.4 × kills) / enc)
trust       = clamp01(peaceful / enc) × (1 − aggression)
eyeGuard    = clamp01(((crits + 1) / (hits + 30)) × 2.2 − 0.1)
keepAway    = clamp01(((closeKills + 0.3 × melee) / (kills + 6)) × 1.6)
jink        = clamp01((longKills / (kills + 6)) × 1.6)
podGuard    = clamp01(pods / (1.5 × raids + 6))
growth      = clamp01(raids / 20)

space       = lerp(4, 9, aggression) × (1 − 0.4 × trust)     metres
patience    = lerp(2.6, 0.7, aggression)                     seconds
shootFirst  = aggression > 0.7
rally       = lerp(30, 75, aggression)                       metres
calm        = lerp(12, 45, aggression)                       seconds

count       = round(1 + 9 × growth)                          structures
bulwarks    = round(count × (0.35 + 0.5 × aggression))       (sites with heat ≥ 0.5, at least 1)
cradles     = count − bulwarks placed
crit damage reduction from shutters = eyeGuard × 35%
```

Site ranking adds a random 0–1 to heat or calm, so ties shuffle.

## 5. Edge Cases

- **New raider** (all zero): aggression 0, trust 0, space 4 m, patience 2.6 s,
  1 structure.
- **Edited save**: all counters clamped (`cleanMemory`); bad zone keys dropped.
- **No zone heat anywhere**: still at least one Bulwark, at the hottest site.
- **Zone cap reached**: new zones stop recording; existing ones still decay.
- **Aggression only rises through `provoked` and kills**; trust needs peaceful
  meetings and is scaled down by aggression, so a violent history caps trust.

## 6. Dependencies

- `husk-ai.md`: consumes every trait.
- `raid-loop.md`: raid start (decay, structure planning) and end (peace logging).
- `combat.md`: hits, crits, kills, melee and grenades feed the memory.
- `economy.md`: pods and wrecks looted; Cradle pods are loot.
- Save system (future ADR): memory persists with the account.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A fresh memory gives aggression 0, space 4 m, patience 2.6 s, 1 structure.
- [ ] provoked = 8, kills = 0, seen = 6 gives aggression 0.8 and shootFirst true.
- [ ] 20 raids give 10 structures.
- [ ] Heat decays to 85% per raid start; zones under 0.2 are removed.
- [ ] Eye-hit-heavy play (crits/hits ≈ 0.5 over 100 hits) fits shutters (eyeGuard > 0.35).
- [ ] A saved memory with out-of-range values loads clamped.
