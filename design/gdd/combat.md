## 1. Overview

Third-person shooting against Husks. The raider carries two gun slots, an
always-available stick, frag grenades, and a shield. Every Husk has one
critical spot (its eye or core); body hits are weaker. Guns wear out with use,
and loose aim gets punished by spread and bloom.

### 3.2 Firing

1. Aim ray from the camera finds the aim point; shots leave the muzzle toward it.
2. Each pellet deviates by a random angle inside the current spread cone
   (uniform over the disc: `r = sqrt(rand) × spread`).
3. Each shot wears the gun by its `wear` value. At 0 durability it cannot fire.
4. Recoil kicks the view and adds bloom; bloom decays at 6°/s.

### 3.3 Stick (melee)

Always carried, no slot, can't be dropped. Q, or the trigger while holstered.
24 damage, 2.4 m reach, 70° arc, 0.65 s cooldown, hit lands 0.42 s into a
0.55 s swing, costs 10 stamina, 2.2 knockback, 0.7 s stagger, 0.3 armour
piercing.

### 3.4 Frag grenade

G to throw. 110 damage in a 7 m radius, 0.5 armour piercing, falling off to
40% at the edge. It also hurts the raider (see Formulas).

### 3.5 Shields

Equip in the shield slot. They absorb a share of incoming damage until their
charge runs out, and **do not regenerate**: only Shield Cells (key 4, +45)
or Recharge in the stash refill them.

| Shield | Capacity | Absorb |
|---|---|---|
| Light | 40 | 50% |
| Medium | 70 | 60% |
| Heavy | 110 | 70% |

### 3.6 Taking damage

- Dodging (roll > 0.15 s remaining) halves incoming damage.
- The shield absorbs first, then health takes the rest.
- Heals: Bandage +25 HP (2.2 s), Herbal Med Kit +60 HP (3.5 s).

## 4. Formulas

- **Spread (degrees)** = `base × (durability < 25 ? 1.5 : 1) × (1 + speed × 0.12) × (airborne ? 2 : 1) × (crouch ? 0.7 : 1) + bloom`,
  where `base = ads ? spreadAds : spreadHip` and `speed` is horizontal m/s.
- **Bloom per shot** = `min(bloom + recoil × 0.35, 6)`; decay `6 °/s`.
- **View kick** = `recoil × (ads ? 0.6 : 1) × (crouch ? 0.8 : 1)`.
- **Range falloff**: `fall = d > range/2 ? 1 − ((d − range/2) / (range/2)) × 0.5 : 1`
  (minimum 50% at max range).
- **Damage to a Husk**:
  `armor = weak ? 0 : def.armor × (1 − AP)`
  `dealt = amount × fall × (1 − armor) × mult`
  then `× bodyMult` (0.6 for neutral Husks) on a body hit, or
  `× (1 − eyeGuard × 0.35)` on an eye hit against a neutral Husk.
  `mult` comes from the hitbox (cores on Pouncer/Warden take extra).
- **Grenade on a Husk**: `damage × (1 − min(1, d / (radius + targetRadius)) × 0.6)`, AP 0.5.
- **Grenade on the raider** (any explosion, including their own): `damage × (1 − d / radius × 0.7)`
  when within radius and in line of sight. Explosions make noise in a 60 m radius.
- **Shield absorb**: `absorbed = min(charge, incoming × absorb)`;
  `health −= incoming − absorbed`; `shieldDurability −= absorbed × 0.12`.

## 5. Edge Cases

- **Gun at 0 durability**: cannot fire; must be repaired (`economy.md`).
- **Shield at 0 durability**: stops absorbing even if charged.
- **Boar at long range**: 9 pellets each lose up to 50% past 17.5 m and
  stop at 35 m.
- **Grenade on the raider**: self-damage applies (up to 110, 33 at the edge),
  blocked by cover. Shield and dodge still reduce it.
- **Armour piercing on a weak spot**: no effect; weak spots already ignore armour.

## 6. Dependencies

- `husk-ai.md`: hitboxes, `bodyMult`, armour, reaction to damage.
- `husk-network.md`: `eyeGuard` lowers crit damage; hits and kills are recorded.
- `player-movement.md`: ADS slows movement, crouch tightens spread, dodge halves damage.
- `economy.md`: ammo, durability, repair, weapon sources.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A Mule eye hit on an unshuttered Skitter does 16; a body hit does 9.6.
- [ ] A shot at max range does 50% of a point-blank shot.
- [ ] A gun under 25 durability has 1.5× base spread.
- [ ] A Light Shield at full charge takes 50% of a 20-damage hit (10 to health).
- [ ] Dodging halves damage before the shield applies.
- [ ] Shields never recharge without a Shield Cell or Recharge action.
