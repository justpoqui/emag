## 1. Overview

Everything the raider owns comes from loot, traders or the Workbench, and
everything carried topside can be lost. Containers roll from weighted tables;
loot sells for credits or breaks down into materials; materials craft gear and
repair worn weapons and shields. Traders sell at 2.5× what they pay, which
makes crafting and salvage the efficient route and credits the convenient one.

## 3. Detailed Design

### 3.1 Inventory

- Loadout: 2 weapon slots, 1 shield slot, 16 bag slots, 1 safe slot.
- Stash: 80 slots. Starting credits: 1200.
- Items stack up to their `stack` value (ammo 25–80, materials 10–30, gear 1).
- Every item has a weight (ammo per round); see `config.js WEIGHTS`.
- **Free kit** when broke: Mule (full mag), Light Shield (full), 60 light
  ammo, 3 bandages.

### 3.2 Item families

Weapons (6), ammo (4), shields (3), heals (2), Shield Cell, Frag, materials
(10, from Scrap Metal to Husk Heart), valuables (6, sell-only, from Rusted
Gear 60 to Gilded Relay 2200). Rarity: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
Full stats: `combat.md` and `config.js ITEMS`.

### 3.3 Containers and loot

- Six container tables: Supply Crate, Locker, Med Cabinet, Weapon Case, Husk
  Pod, Car Trunk (`LOOT_TABLES`). Each rolls 1–4 stacks from weighted entries.
- Each raid every container spot is filled with 60% chance (`CONTAINER_FILL`)
  with a random type from its pool. Camp caches and survivor shacks are always
  filled; Cradle pods exist only where a Cradle stands.
- Richer drop conditions can add one extra stack (see Formulas).
- Weapons and shields found topside are worn: 35–90% durability.
- Destroyed Husks leave wrecks rolled from their `drops` table.
- Searching reveals items one at a time; pinned-recipe needs are marked.

### 3.4 Traders (`TRADERS`)

- **Tobias** (gunsmith): all 6 guns, all ammo, frags.
- **Nia** (medic): bandages, med kits, cells, all 3 shields.
- **Pell**: buys loot from the stash.
- Buy price = value × 2.5. Sell price = value (worn gear less).

**DESIGN CALL (delegated): the 2.5× markup is intentional.** It is the main
credit sink and keeps crafting worth doing.

### 3.5 Workbench

Sections: Weapons, Ammo, Shields, Medical, Explosives, Repair, Salvage.
16 recipes (`RECIPES`), e.g. 3 Fabric → 2 Bandages; Scrap 18 + Husk Plating 8
+ Husk Circuitry 3 + Husk Heart 1 → Heron. Recipes can be **pinned** to show
needs on the hub and raid HUD.

### 3.6 Durability and repair

- Max 100. Guns lose `wear` per shot (0.07 Sparrow – 1.1 Heron). Shields lose
  0.12 per point absorbed.
- Under 25 a gun's spread is × 1.5; at 0 it can't fire / a shield stops absorbing.
- Repair per 25 missing points: guns 2 Scrap + 1 Rubber (+1 Husk Plating for
  rare and above); shields 1 Fabric + 1 Husk Plating. Or pay credits.

### 3.7 Salvage

Dismantle (right-click or the Salvage bench) into the materials in `SALVAGE`.
Basic materials can't be broken down. Worn gear yields less (minimum half).

## 4. Formulas

- **Roll count** = `randInt(rolls.min, rolls.max)`; each roll picks an entry
  with probability `weight / Σweights` and a count `randInt(min, max)`.
- **Extra stack**: with probability `lootMul − 1` (Night 0.4, Storm 0.2), one
  more rolled stack.
- **Buy** = `ceil(value × 2.5 × qty)`
- **Sell** = `value × n`, or for weapons/shields `round(value × n × (0.4 + 0.6 × dur/100))`
- **Repair (credits)** = `value × 0.35 × missing/100`
- **Repair (materials)** = recipe × `ceil(missing / 25)` (charged per 25-point step)
- **Salvage** = `floor(base × count × wear)`, `wear = 0.5 + 0.5 × dur/100` (1 for non-durable)

## 5. Edge Cases

- **Stash full**: crafting is refused up front ("Stash is full."). Buying is
  refused only when *nothing* fits. **Bug found:** when part of a multi-unit
  purchase fits (e.g. ammo bundles), the full price is charged and the rest is
  lost (`ui.js` buy handler). The port should refuse the purchase unless all
  of it fits.
- **Selling a broken (0%) weapon**: still 40% of value.
- **Salvage of a near-zero item**: floors may give nothing; returns null.
- **Arbitrage check**: buy at 2.5×, salvage, craft, sell. No loop found
  profitable at current values, but `/balance-check` should confirm.
- **Edited save**: loaded saves are clamped to values the game can produce.

## 6. Dependencies

- `raid-loop.md`: loot comes from raids; death loses the loadout.
- `combat.md`: ammo use, weapon wear, shield wear.
- `husk-network.md`: Cradles add pods; pod looting is recorded.
- `sanctuary-hub.md`: where trading and crafting happen.
- Save system (future ADR).

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A Supply Crate always holds 2–4 rolls of its table's items.
- [ ] Buying a Badger costs 2250 credits; selling a pristine one gives 900.
- [ ] A Badger at 50% durability sells for 630.
- [ ] Repairing a gun from 40 to 100 costs 3 steps of materials.
- [ ] Dismantling a 0% Heron gives half its salvage list (floored).
- [ ] Guns found topside have 35–90 durability.
