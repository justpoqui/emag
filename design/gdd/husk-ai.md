## 1. Overview

Husks are the machines of the Crucible AI. Three kinds (Skitter, Buzzer,
Watcher) are **neutral but cautious**: they watch the raider, keep their
distance, warn, and only fight when crowded, aimed at for too long, or shot.
Two kinds (Pouncer, Warden) attack on sight. Husks see only ahead and only so
far; crouching, bushes and broken line of sight let the raider slip away.
Together with `husk-network.md` this is Scrapfall's core pillar.

## 3. Detailed Design

Neutral Husks take 60% damage away from the eye (`bodyMult` 0.6). Pouncer
and Warden have a core that takes extra damage (the Warden's: triple).
Destroyed Buzzers and Watchers break into falling debris. Every destroyed Husk
leaves a lootable wreck rolled from its `drops` table.

### 3.2 States

- **idle**: going about its business.
- **wary** (neutral only): has noticed the raider. Watches, keeps
  `space` metres away, builds **tension** while crowded or aimed at.
- **alert**: hostile. Attacks, chases the last seen position, searches.

Transitions:
1. Seeing the raider: non-neutral → alert (10 s timer). Neutral idle → wary
   (8 s timer), or straight to alert if the network trait `shootFirst` is
   set and the raider is within 60% of sight range.
2. Wary tension rises 1/s while the raider is within `space`, +0.6/s while
   aiming at it, and falls 0.6/s otherwise. At 45% of `patience` it warns
   (chirp, eye flare); at 100% it turns alert.
3. Wary with no sight for 8 s → idle, and logs a **peaceful meeting** if it
   was never hostile.
4. Being hit, or a nearby first strike (within `rally` metres), → alert.
5. Alert with no sight when the 10 s timer runs out → wary (neutral) or idle.
   Neutral Husks also stand down to wary after `calm` seconds without
   trouble, unless the raider is in sight and inside `space`.
6. Lost sight while alert: a **?** shows while it searches round the last
   seen position (`SEARCH_SECONDS` 7), then gives up.
7. Noise (gunfire, explosions 60 m, lift calls 90 m) alerts Husks in range
   toward the source without making them hostile.

### 3.3 Perception

- Sees within `sight × conditionSight × (crouching ? 0.6 : 1)` metres.
- Only inside its FOV cone, except within `SENSE_RADIUS` 3 m (any direction).
- Needs terrain/structure line of sight; Skitters look from 0.35 m higher.
- Bushes block sight beyond 3 m (low scrub hides a crouched raider; shrubs and
  thickets hide a standing one).
- Watchers see only inside their lamp beam.

### 3.4 Skitter latch (`LATCH`)

A leap that connects latches with 33% chance. Each latched Skitter drains
1 HP/s and cuts stamina regen by 15%. Sprinting for 0.9–1.6 s throws one off;
each has a 15% chance to poison on release (10 damage over 5 s).

### 3.5 Network counters used by the AI

- Skitters keep `space × (1 + keepAway × 0.6)`; others keep `space × 1.5`.
- `keepAway > 0.35`: melee-range attackers hold back from stick reach.
- Buzzers orbit at `16 + keepAway × 6` m and strafe `(1 + jink)` faster.
- Eye shutters close to `eyeGuard` (60% of it while wary).

## 4. Formulas

- `effectiveSight = sight × conditionSight × (crouch ? 0.6 : 1)`
- `inFov = dot(facing, toRaider) ≥ cos(fov / 2)` (skipped within 3 m)
- `tension += dt × (crowded + 0.6 × aimedAt)`; else `tension −= 0.6 × dt`
- Warn at `tension > patience × 0.45`; attack at `tension > patience`
- Personal space: `space` (Skitter: `× (1 + keepAway × 0.6)`; others `× 1.5`)
- Damage taken: see `combat.md` §4.

## 5. Edge Cases

- **Raider dies:** all Husks drop to wary (neutral) or idle.
- **Aiming without crowding:** a neutral Husk eventually attacks
  (0.6 tension/s against 0.7–2.6 s patience), so long staring provokes.
- **Shooting a neutral Husk from out of its sight:** still a first strike;
  nearby neutrals within `rally` turn hostile.
- **Several latched Skitters:** drain and regen cuts stack per Skitter.
- **Husk pod looted:** neutrals within 25 m are alerted, hostile if
  `podGuard > 0.3` or `aggression > 0.5`.

## 6. Dependencies

- `husk-network.md`: all trait values (`space`, `patience`, `rally`,

- `combat.md`: damage resolution and weak spots.
- `raid-loop.md`: spawning, reinforcements, lift-call attackers, drop-condition sight.
- `player-movement.md`: crouch, sprint (shaking off latches).
- World: line of sight, bushes, spawn points.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A standing raider 17 m in front of a Skitter is not seen; 15 m is.
- [ ] A crouched raider at 10 m in front of a Skitter is not seen (9.6 m limit).
- [ ] A raider behind a Husk is not seen beyond 3 m and is seen within 3 m.
- [ ] A neutral Husk crowded for `patience` seconds turns hostile and warns first.
- [ ] Shooting one neutral Husk turns other neutrals within `rally` hostile.
- [ ] Pouncers and Wardens attack on first sight.
- [ ] A latched Skitter drains 1 HP/s until sprinted off.
