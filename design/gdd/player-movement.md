## 1. Overview

Third-person movement for the raider: walk, sprint, crouch, jump and a dodge
roll, governed by a stamina pool and slowed by what the raider carries.
Holstering the gun trades readiness for speed.

## 3. Detailed Design

### 3.1 Values (`PLAYER`)

| Value | Amount |
|---|---|
| Health | 100 |
| Stamina | 100 |
| Walk speed | 4.6 m/s |
| Sprint speed | 7.4 m/s |
| Crouch speed | 2.4 m/s |
| Aim-down-sights speed | 3.0 m/s |
| Jump velocity | 6.2 m/s |
| Sprint drain | 16 stamina/s |
| Stamina regen | 22/s |
| Dodge roll cost | 28 stamina |

### 3.2 Rules

1. **Sprint** needs: moving forward, not aiming, stamina > 1, not using an
   item, no stamina lock, and carried load ≤ 100%. Running stamina dry while
   sprinting locks sprint and regen for 1.2 s.
2. **Dodge roll** (V or Left Alt): needs to be on the ground, not already
   rolling, and stamina ≥ 14 (half the cost). Lasts 0.55 s, costs 28 stamina,
   then locks regen 0.8 s. While more than 0.15 s of it remains, damage taken
   is halved.
3. **Crouch** (C): slower, quieter, harder to see (Husk sight × 0.6), tighter
   spread (× 0.7), lower eye height (1.05 m vs 1.6 m).
4. **Carry weight**: `CARRY_LIMIT` 35 kg over the whole loadout. Over 75%
   slows the raider; over 100% slows a lot and blocks sprinting.
5. **Holstered** (H or X), or with no gun: × 1.12 speed; the trigger swings the stick.
6. **Using an item** (heal, cell): movement slowed for the use time.
7. **Latched Skitters** cut stamina regen 15% each (`husk-ai.md` §3.4).

## 4. Formulas

- `load = loadoutWeight / 35`
- `speedMul = load > 1 ? 0.7 : load > 0.75 ? 1 − (load − 0.75) × 0.6 : 1`
  (so 0.85 at exactly 100%, then a step down to 0.7 over it)
- `speedMul ×= 1.12` when holstered or unarmed
- `stamina −= 16 × dt` while sprinting; else `stamina += 22 × regenMul × dt`
  when not locked, with `regenMul = max(0, 1 − 0.15 × latchedSkitters)`

## 5. Edge Cases

- **Exactly at 100% load**: speed 0.85 and sprint allowed; 35.01 kg drops to 0.7
  with no sprint. The step is abrupt. `INTENT UNKNOWN` whether a smooth curve
  was meant.
- **Rolling with under 28 stamina** (but ≥ 14): allowed, stamina floors at 0.
- **Seven or more latched Skitters**: regen is clamped at 0, never negative.
- **Shaking off latches needs sprinting**, which an overloaded raider cannot do.
  Overloaded + latched means no escape but killing the Skitters.
