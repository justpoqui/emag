## 1. Overview

Between raids the raider lives in the Patron's Sanctuary, an old mine shaft
turned survivalist town under the Dustline. The hub is both a menu (stash,
loadout, traders, Workbench, Raider Log) and a walkable place where those
services are rooms and people. It is the safe half of the loop: everything
here is kept.

## 3. Detailed Design

### 3.1 Screens

- **Title screen** (`home.js`): logo and main menu over a live view of the
  Dustline, raider card, Field Bulletin (news and admin posts), sign-in.
- **Hub screen**: stash and loadout on the left, the raider on the right.
  **EXPLORE THE SANCTUARY** switches to walking.

### 3.2 Rooms (metres; floor y = 0, ceiling 3.4)

| Room | Where | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| Quarters | x −4..4, z −3.5..3.5 | Stash locker; second weapon on the rack; full-length mirror (wardrobe) on the east wall |
| Hallway | x −5..33, z −6.6..−3.6 | Doors to every room; lift at the east end |
| Trade Hall | x −0.5..9, z −15..−6.6 | Tobias, Nia, Pell (walk up, press E) |
| Workshop | x 9..19, z −15..−6.6 | One bench per Workbench section |
| Checkout | x 19..28.5, z −15..−6.6 | Pick the drop condition, with a live view |
| Lift | x 33..36.5, z −6.6..−3.6 | Hold E to deploy |

Tab opens the stash and loadout anywhere; Esc opens the Sanctuary menu.

### 3.3 Wardrobe (`WARDROBE`)

At the mirror (E): shirt (Field Jacket, Work Tee), trousers (Cargo Pants,
Utility Shorts), hat (Work Cap, Boonie Hat, none), hair (Short Crop,
Ponytail). The change shows in the mirror at once and is worn topside.
Cosmetic only.

### 3.4 Other hub features

- Raider Log: the Husk Network read-out (`husk-network.md` §3.4).
- Pinned recipes show needs against stash + bag.
- Shield recharge and dismantle from the stash context menu.
- Menus and pause run at 30 fps to save power.

## 4. Formulas

None found in the source: the hub has no numeric rules of its own. Prices,
recipes and repair costs belong to `economy.md`.

## 5. Edge Cases

- **Deploying with an empty loadout**: allowed (free kit offered when broke).
- **Leaving explore mode mid-conversation**: `INTENT UNKNOWN`; confirm the
  trader window closes cleanly during the port.
- **Wardrobe while signed out / local account**: outfit saved in the local profile.

## 6. Dependencies

- `economy.md`: everything sold, crafted, repaired or salvaged.
- `raid-loop.md`: drop selection and deploy.
- `husk-network.md`: Raider Log.
- Rig and outfit models (`rig.js`, `outfit.js`): to be replaced by Blender
  models in the Godot port.
- Accounts and save (future ADR).

## 8. Acceptance Criteria
