**One-sentence pitch:** A browser extraction shooter where the machines you raid against remember how you fought them, and change their behaviour, their defences and the map itself in response.

## Core loop
- **Prepare** in the Sanctuary: put together a loadout from the stash, buy from Tobias/Nia, and craft, repair or salvage at the Workbench.
- **Raid** the Dustline (Golden Hour / Night / Storm): search containers and Husk wrecks, then fight or sneak past Husks, which stay neutral until provoked.
- **Extract** at one of four lifts (a 20 s call that draws Husks, then 25 s open) before the 20-minute clock runs out. Bring the loot home and sell or craft with it.
- **The Husk Network learns** from every raid, so the next drop plays differently: your disposition, Husk counters (eye shutters, keeping out of reach, jinking, guarding pods) and topside Bulwarks/Cradles.

## Player goal & fail state — what "working" looks like
- Goal: extract carrying more value than you deployed with (`gained = extracted loadout value − start value`), and grow your stash/credits across raids.
- Fail: death, or still topside when the clock hits 0. The whole loadout is lost except the 1-slot safe pocket, and you get half the raid XP. Closing the game mid-raid counts as death.

## MVP — what must exist to be the game
All implemented as of `e39543a`:
- Raid lifecycle: deploy → loot → call lift → extract or lose the loadout (`game.js`).
- Third-person combat: 6 guns + stick + frags, ADS, recoil/bloom, dodge, crouch, durability (`weapons.js`, `player.js`).
- Five Husk types with sight cones, a 3 m sense radius, a 7 s search, cover that blocks sight, and eye crits (body hits ×0.6) (`enemies.js`).
- **The Husk Network**, the core pillar: memory across raids → disposition, counters and topside infrastructure (`network.js`, `infrastructure.js`).
- Sanctuary hub: 80-slot stash, traders (buy at value × 2.5, sell at value), Workbench recipes, repair, salvage (`sanctuary.js`, `ui.js`).
- Randomised containers drawn from 6 weighted loot tables; the map condition scales Husks, loot and sight (`config.js` `LOOT_TABLES`, `MAPS`).
- Saved profile: accounts plus a local/cloud save, clamped on load (`accounts.js`, `inventory.js`).
